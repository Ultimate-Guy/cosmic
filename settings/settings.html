Settings

Customize Cosmic on this browser. Your choices are saved locally and applied across Cosmic pages.

Appearance

Choose the colors and atmosphere used by Cosmic.

Background

Use a custom image and optionally enable the interactive background effect.

Images are stored locally in your browser and are limited to about 2.5 MB.

Custom Crosshair

Choose the on-screen crosshair style. Saved in this browser and applied across Cosmic pages.

Interface

Control visual effects across Cosmic.

Background Music

Choose an uploaded MP3 and control playback across Cosmic pages.

No bundled Cosmic track is installed yet. Upload an MP3 to use background music.

Tab Cloak

Choose a preset with a title and icon preview. Cosmic keeps the preset locally in this browser.

These are original Cosmic-made icons inspired by common school/productivity tab styles; no third-party site assets are bundled.

Panic Shortcut

Choose a keyboard key and destination for a quick navigation shortcut.

The shortcut only navigates to the URL you choose. It does not hide or delete browser history.

Data

Remove Cosmic preferences stored in this browser.

This resets themes, background preferences, music preferences, and other Cosmic local settings. It does not delete your files.
